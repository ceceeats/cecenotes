Ogee lozengeA diamond (lozenge) whose four sides each make an S curve, the ogee. Also sold as a Moorish or Moroccan diamond.
Tube bailThe fixed loop on top. Seen from the front it looks like a narrow tab; the chain runs left to right through it, so the face never flips.
BeadsThe little gold balls on the points. Goldsmiths also call them granules.
Rim and sunken faceA raised border around a lower, blank middle. The second line inside is an inner frame.
