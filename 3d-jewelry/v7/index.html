Drag a dot to reshape. Tap the line between dots to add one. Delete dots: turn it on, tap the dots you want gone, tap it again to stop. Corner on/off: tap a dot first, then this turns that dot into a sharp point (like a heart tip) or back to a smooth curve.

Window

A window is a hole right through the metal: a key bow, a donut, or an open frame. Drag its centre dot on the grid to move it.

Border: a raised band just inside the edge. Inner line: a thin raised line inside the border. Like pin 1.

Shell ribs

Grooves that fan out from the bottom centre, like a scallop shell. 0 turns them off.

Hammered

On the grid: drag the cross to move a mark, drag the small ring above it to turn it. Marks always stay inside the margin.

Drag the red dot on the grid to move the ring along the edge. The shape stays the same; the loop grows out of the edge wherever you drop it. With the padlock arch on, the arch is the bail and there is no extra ring.

Padlock arch

Drag the arch's dots on the grid: the top dot sets its height and position, the side dots set its width.

Classic signet: a flat table in your face shape, walls that melt into the shoulders, one smooth piece. On the grid, up and down runs along the finger.

Gold balls are tiny round beads soldered onto the edge, like the ones on the points of pin 1.
